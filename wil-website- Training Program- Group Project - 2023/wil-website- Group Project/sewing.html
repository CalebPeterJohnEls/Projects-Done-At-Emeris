Sewing

Sewing is the craft of fastening or attaching objects using stitches made with a sewing needle and thread. Sewing is one of the oldest of the textile arts, arising in the Paleolithic era. Sewing is stitching cloth, leather, furs, or other materials, using a needle and thread. The four main types of sewing are machine stitching, overlocking, serging, and hand stitching. Hand stitching is the most basic type of sewing, and it is done with a needle and thread. It's commonly used for small jobs like buttoning clothes or doing repairs. It allows you to express yourself and your creativity through colours and design. Every aspect of this activity allows you to be creative while designing your own clothing. It also gives you something to be proud of as your creations can make you stand out from the rest.
