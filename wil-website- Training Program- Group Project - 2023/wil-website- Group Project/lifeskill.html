Life
Skills

Life skills are the fundamental skills and abilities someone require to deal with day-to-day obstacles and objectives. They involve social, emotional, and practical skills that are essential for success, well-being, and personal development. These abilities include learning the basics of reading and arithmetic, comprehending labour regulations, and being financially literate. They are essential for independence, self-sufficiency, fostering healthy interpersonal bonds, making wise judgements, and adjusting to a world that is always changing. Life skills are the cornerstone of human growth, enhancing well-being and equipping people with the courage to act on opportunities and confront obstacles head-on.
