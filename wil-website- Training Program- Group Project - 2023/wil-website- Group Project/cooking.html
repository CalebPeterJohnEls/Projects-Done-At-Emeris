Cooking

Regardless of whether you want to be a Michelin star chef, a professional meal prepper or next-level home cook, having a few technical food courses under your apron belt will stand you in good stead. Yes, preparing food is all about the practical hands-on experience of processing and cooking ingredients but it is also about important theoretical knowledge like knowing safe food practices, how to identify different cuts of meat or knowing the difference between lobsters and crayfish! That’s where Empowering the Nation comes in. We’ve reached out to our publishers for their best courses on food hygiene, food and beverage management, and vegan diets. In return, you get the foundational knowledge you need for different cooking methods and running a kitchen or restaurant.
